# 点 · 线 · 面 → 动 — Claude Motion Reel (15 s)

**Format** 1920×1080 · 60 fps · 900 frames · 15.000 s · original score 128 BPM, 8 bars (1 bar = 1.875 s, 1 beat = 0.469 s)

**Idea** Kandinsky's *Point and Line to Plane* — the same 点线面 that opens every Chinese design
course (平面构成) — is the whole grammar of motion design. The film starts with one red point and
grows it, one bar at a time, into line, plane, type, flow, volume and rhythm, then stamps it into a
seal: 动 (motion). Each chapter is born *from* the previous one; there are no "next slide" cuts. The
last frame returns to the first, so the reel loops seamlessly.

**Palette** Ink `#0B0B0D` · Paper `#EDE8DF` · Vermilion `#FF3B1F` (Bauhaus red = seal red) ·
Ultramarine `#2440FF` · Signal yellow `#FFC21A`

**Type** Archivo variable (wght 100–900, wdth 62–125) · JetBrains Mono (HUD) · Noto Sans CJK SC

**HUD** crop marks, chapter index with rolling numerals, running SMPTE timecode + frame counter,
bar/beat indicator, timeline scrubber. Colour adapts per pixel to the background underneath.

| Bar | Time | Chapter | What happens | Transition out (object continuity) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.000–1.875 | 01 点 POINT | A red dot hops three times on the beat, gaining height: anticipation, squash & stretch, slow-in/slow-out. Onion-skin ghosts show the spacing; annotations name the principles. | The dot squashes, then fires right; its trail **becomes the line**. |
| 2 | 1.875–3.750 | 02 线 LINE | The line snaps full-width and vibrates like a plucked string (follow-through), then fans into 27 lines carrying phase-offset waves — a woven ribbon. | Lines thicken until they merge into a paper **plane**; the red line contracts to a 280 px segment. |
| 3 | 3.750–5.625 | 03 面 PLANE | The segment extrudes into a red square; a blue circle rolls in, a yellow triangle drops in. Riso overprint (multiply), halftone and misregistered outlines. | The square grows to **become the frame**, pushing the other shapes out. |
| 4 | 5.625–7.500 | 04 字 TYPE | "EVERY / FRAME / IS A / DECISION." builds a justified poster — every line is the same width by animating the **wdth/wght axes** and tracking, with live axis read-outs. Vertical 中文 subtitle. Riser shake. | The full stop is the point again — it **detonates**. |
| 5 | 7.500–9.375 | 05 流 FLOW | DROP. Glyphs shatter into 6,000 particles; a shock ring wipes to ink; a curl-noise field turns them into glowing streams that pulse with the kick. | Particles are pulled onto the surface of a **3D sphere**. |
| 6 | 9.375–11.250 | 06 体 VOLUME | A lit point-cloud morphs sphere → cube → pyramid on the beats (the 2D circle/square/triangle, now 3D), with snap-rotations, wireframes, orbit ring and floor grid. | The camera dollies **through** the cloud — warp streaks — flash. |
| 7 | 11.250–13.125 | 07 律 RHYTHM | Beat-cut pattern cards, each entering with a different transition: iris, diagonal wipe, tile-flip, slice-slide (op-art rings, stripes, shape grid, 律). | Everything **implodes** into a single point. |
| 8 | 13.125–15.000 | 08 动 MOTION | The point slams down as a vermilion seal 「动」 on paper — ink splash, shock ring — beside "MOTION DESIGN / CLAUDE — REEL 2026" with logo-construction guides and the chapter row 点线面字流体律动. | Iris closes back to the red dot on ink = frame 0. **Loop.** |

**Sound** All synthesized in code and locked to the same beat grid as the picture: tick + pitched
bounces (bar 1), Karplus-Strong string pluck (bar 2), kick enters with the square (bar 3), word stabs +
riser + snare roll (bar 4), drop with sub, clap and particle shimmer (bars 5–6), stutter hits per card
and reverse swell (bar 7), seal impact with long tail and a final tick that matches the opening tick (bar 8).
